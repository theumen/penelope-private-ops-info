Penelope Emeralds

Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026.

Purpose and Google data

Penelope Emeralds is a private personal-assistant integration. After the account holder grants OAuth permission, it may access Gmail messages and settings, calendars, and Drive files in the connected Google account. It uses that access to read, organize, create, edit or send information only as needed for requested assistant tasks. The connected account's owner controls which tasks are authorized; an OAuth grant alone does not authorize contacting third parties.

Processing and storage

Google data is processed in the owner's private assistant environment. Content relevant to a task may be sent to configured AI services for that task and may appear in private task outputs, local operational records, and configured backups. Data is not placed on this public website. The desktop OAuth client secret and authorization tokens are kept in macOS Keychain, not in this repository.

Services and use restrictions

Google supplies authentication and account APIs. Configured AI and communication services may process data needed to complete an authorized task under their own service policies. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used by this application to train generalized AI models. Penelope Emeralds' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and control

Task outputs and operational records may remain in the owner's private workspace and backups until removed under the owner's retention practices. The account holder can revoke future access through Google Account connections. Revocation does not automatically erase previously created local records, backups or delivered messages; those must be removed separately. For a privacy question or deletion request, use the support email on the application's Google consent screen.

This website

This static site has no account sign-in, forms, application analytics or advertising scripts. GitHub Pages may process ordinary web requests under GitHub's own privacy policy.
