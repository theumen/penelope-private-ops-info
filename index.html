Penelope Emeralds

Penelope Emeralds is a privately operated personal assistant integration for its own Google account. It is not a public service and has no general sign-up.

Google account features

With the account holder's OAuth consent, the desktop integration can manage Gmail messages and settings, calendars, and Drive files to carry out requested assistant tasks. These are broad permissions on the connected account. Access to other people's accounts is not offered.

This public information site has no access to Google data or the assistant runtime. No messages, files, credentials or private operational records are hosted here.
